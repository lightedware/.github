# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for each person. We welcome
each person, we speak with respect, and we do not accept discrimination.

## Our standards

Do this:

- Show empathy and kindness.
- Respect a different opinion and a different experience.
- Give feedback with care, and accept feedback with grace.
- Accept responsibility for your mistake, apologize, and learn from it.
- Think about what is best for the community, and not only for you.

Do not do this:

- Sexual language, sexual images, or sexual attention of any type.
- An insult, a personal attack, or a political attack.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publication of the private information of a different person, such as an address, with no
  permission from that person.
- Other behavior that is not correct in a professional space.

## Scope

This code applies in each community space of Lightedware. It also applies when a person represents
Lightedware in public, as an example with an official email address, or with an official social
media account.

## Reports

Report unacceptable behavior to `security@lightedware.com`. We examine each report quickly and
fairly, and we keep the identity of the reporter private.

## Consequences

A leader of the community can remove or reject a contribution that breaks this code. A leader can
also give a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban. The consequence matches how serious the
behavior is, and the leader gives the reason for it.
